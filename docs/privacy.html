Lot Scanner — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Lot Scanner is an internal tool for RetroStoreManager store employees to photograph a customer's game lot and get an AI-assisted price quote. This policy covers what the app collects and why.

Who uses this app

Lot Scanner is used by store employees, not by their customers. You sign in with the same employee account you use for RetroStoreManager.

What we collect

  • Employee login — your RetroStoreManager email is used to authenticate you and identify which store you work for. We do not collect a separate password for this app; it uses your existing RetroStoreManager credentials.
  • Photos — photos you take of a game lot are uploaded to identify the games in it and estimate a price. Photos are sent to Anthropic (the maker of Claude, the AI model used for identification) for processing, and stored so you can review and correct the results before confirming a purchase.
  • Usage data — standard application logs (e.g. errors, request timing) for keeping the service running reliably. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

What we don't collect

Lot Scanner does not collect any information about your store's customers. The games in a scanned lot and their estimated prices are not linked to a customer identity by this app.

Camera and photo library access

The app requests camera access to photograph a lot, and photo library access if you choose an existing photo instead of taking a new one. Photos are only used for the lot-scanning feature described above.

Data retention

Photos and scan results are retained for as long as your store's RetroStoreManager account is active, so past scans remain reviewable. You can ask your store administrator to request deletion of specific scan records.

Third parties

Photos are shared with Anthropic solely to identify the games in them. We do not sell or share your data for advertising.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to your RetroStoreManager account administrator.
